Next steps in learning

At each newly completed trading session, the research job retrains its rule comparisons from rolling chronological segments. It saves the selected policy and the results, then queues prospective paper instructions. One historical replay per strategy also shows every simulated entry and exit. The explorer follows a 20-session relative-strength rotation rule even when the learner's filters are inactive, so it can collect a different paper-trading baseline.

No trades are fabricated to fill the journal. If markets are closed, data fails, or orders aren't eligible, there may be no new forward fills. Provider quotes may be delayed or adjusted. This system uses GitHub's free scheduled jobs, not an always-on AI or exchange-grade feed.
