What the engine is evaluating

EMA12 vs EMA48 for trend, RSI14 for momentum, 20-bar breakout or EMA12 recovery, relative volume and ATR volatility. Each failed condition appears below.

BUY orders require all five checks. SELL orders evaluate an ATR trail, EMA trend failure and RSI overextension. Each order is fictional; it can fill only at a later bar's open.

Risk limits
1% equity at risk per planned position; 20% maximum allocation per ticker; 3 positions; $1 fee and 0.1% hypothetical slippage.
